## Transformations made for this catalog

Palette entries were arranged into numbered Draw.io contact sheets. Relative image resources were embedded for offline rendering. Contact sheets were exported to PNG and cropped into individual previews. Derived indexes add IDs, namespaces, categories, relative file paths and workflow metadata. The original upstream shape styles and source attribution remain available. Brand SVGs were downloaded as selected variants for offline display.

The repository's MIT license applies to our original documentation and query/validation code, not to the above third-party material or trademarks. Contributions correcting provenance or asset attribution are welcome.
